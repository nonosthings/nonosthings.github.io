Privacy policy

Your widgets are personal.
Your information stays on your device.

Effective date: October 6, 2026 Last updated: October 6, 2026

WidgetBlocks does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics services, or tracking tools in the app.

About this policy

This policy describes how WidgetBlocks, an app developed by nonosthings, handles information when you create and use custom widgets. It applies to the WidgetBlocks app and its widget extension.

Information used on your device

WidgetBlocks uses the information needed to show the widgets you create. This information is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer or to third-party services by WidgetBlocks.

  • Widget designs and preferences. Your layouts, names, text, colors, font choices, and editor preferences are saved locally. The app and its widget extension share local storage so your saved designs can appear in widgets.
  • Photos you choose. If you add a photo component, the system photo picker lets you select an image. WidgetBlocks stores a resized copy locally for use in your widget. It does not browse your entire photo library or upload the image.
  • Calendar events. If you grant calendar access, WidgetBlocks reads upcoming event information, such as titles, dates, and times, to display it in your widgets.
  • Reminders. If you grant reminder access, WidgetBlocks reads incomplete reminders, including titles and due dates, to display them in your widgets.

WidgetBlocks does not modify your calendar events or reminders.

Your choices and permissions

Calendar and reminder access are optional and requested separately when you add those components. You can decline access and continue using other components. You can change or revoke access at any time in your device’s Settings.

You choose which photos to import. Removing a photo component and saving the design, or deleting a design, removes its local photo copy when no other saved design uses that copy. Your original photo is not deleted.

Information placed in a widget may be visible to anyone who can see your screen. Choose the content and placement of your widgets accordingly.

Storage and deletion

Saved designs and preferences remain on your device until you change or remove them. You can delete saved designs in WidgetBlocks. Removing the app and its data through your device’s system controls removes its locally stored app data. There is no WidgetBlocks account or server-side profile to delete.

Device backups and any syncing of your photo library, calendars, or reminders are managed by your operating system and the services you choose. WidgetBlocks does not operate its own cloud storage or sync service.

Analytics, advertising, and third parties

WidgetBlocks does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or its own remote crash-reporting service. It does not collect device identifiers, usage history, or location data. It does not sell or share personal information.

Apple’s App Store, operating system, and optional diagnostics are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and your Apple privacy settings.

Contacting support

If you choose to email help@nonosthings.tech, the developer receives your email address and whatever you include in your message. That information is used to respond to your request. Please avoid including private photos, calendar details, or reminders unless necessary. Sending a support email is separate from using the app; WidgetBlocks does not send it automatically.

This website

This policy’s no-collection statement describes the WidgetBlocks app. These informational webpages do not add analytics, tracking scripts, forms, or cookies. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose hosting infrastructure may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement. External links are subject to the policies of the sites you visit.

Children’s privacy

WidgetBlocks does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, through use of the app.

Changes to this policy

If WidgetBlocks’ privacy practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will change to reflect the revision.

Questions?

For questions about WidgetBlocks or this privacy policy, contact nonosthings at help@nonosthings.tech.
