A little help

Let’s get you set up.

How do I add my widget?

Create a design in InfoBlocks and tap Save. Add an InfoBlocks widget using your device’s widget gallery, then edit the widget to choose your saved design. You can also open the installation guide on your design’s card in the app.

Do I need to allow calendar or reminder access?

Only if you want those components. You can use clocks, dates, and photos without granting calendar or reminder access, and you can change permissions in your device’s Settings.
